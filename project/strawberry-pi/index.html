Summary

My attempt at making a handheld game console to play retro-games. With this project the main goal is to learn the full design process for a physical product. From product design to hardware design to software.

Details

The project started when I was making a mod for a game. I used an old nintendo 3DS XL to emulate the game but I found that my custom sprites were washed out. When looking for alternatives to play emulated games on I found only android based chinese devices which did not sit well with me. As such I started researching what it would take to develop my own device. After convincing myself this would indeed be possible I began my yourney. I choose the raspberry pi CM5 module as my base platform and dubbed the project strawberry-pi I divided the work into smaller bite size chunks.

  • Audio: Make an pcb to propototype audio support
  • IO: Button, triggers etc
  • Power: Battery power + usb-c switchover and charge
  • Video: Display + led driver

Currently I have most of the electrical prototypes done and have a proof of concept for each. I’m now designing the case where everything sits in, I’ve got the controller design dialed in and am working on the display enclosure. The last step is to merge all my smaller prototypes in a single motherboard and design a slimmer heatsink for the CM5 module.

The end-goal is to make everything public and offer a DIY kit. all components can be 3D printed and all electrical schematics will be made public. After which I will focus on the software to build a custom linux distro for the device.

A big shoutout also goes to the following companies which were willing to help me provide samples for lipo batteries, displays and cover glass

  • Midas Displays: They provided samples for the display module used.
  • Konshen Glass: They provided samples for the cover glass used in the display module.
  • LiPol Battery: They provided samples for the lipo cell used to power the device.
